# `DenseBlockHoppingBlochEvaluation` scientific contract

The record correlates a dense real-space hopping model with its sampled reciprocal
matrix field. It preserves basis, gauge, energy reference, units, and coordinate
ordering through the retained model and coordinate objects.

It makes no claim that matrices are Hermitian, eigenvalues are bands, or sampling is
converged.
