# `ScalarHoppingBlochEvaluation` scientific contract

The record correlates one scalar ordinary-lattice hopping model with its sampled
reciprocal field. It retains the source displacement convention, energy unit, energy
reference, and coordinate order through exact owned records.

Complex values are permitted and are not evidence of a numerical defect. The record
does not establish Hermiticity, spectral convergence, material fidelity, or physical
adequacy.
