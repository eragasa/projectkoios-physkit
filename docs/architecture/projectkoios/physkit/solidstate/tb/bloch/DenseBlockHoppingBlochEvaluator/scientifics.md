# `DenseBlockHoppingBlochEvaluator` scientific contract

The evaluator assembles dense Bloch matrices in the exact basis and energy convention
of the supplied dense hopping model. Family evaluation requires common dimension,
basis identity, basis size, unit, and energy reference.

It does not diagonalize matrices, infer Hermiticity, select bands, truncate support,
or judge scientific adequacy.
