# `HomopolarSp3sStarSpatialBasis` scientific contract

Admission establishes software-level basis structure only. It does not establish that a material is accurately represented by sp3s-star orbitals.

The represented relation

$$
\mathcal B_c=((c,s,0),(c,p_x,0),(c,p_y,0),(c,p_z,0),(c,s,1)),\qquad N_b=5N_c.
$$

is a mathematical or structural software contract. Passing construction and tests
establishes conformance to that contract only; it does not establish material accuracy,
parameter acceptance, convergence, independent verification, or experimental agreement.

It does not admit heteropolar semantics, onsite values, spin, overlap, or hopping parameters.
