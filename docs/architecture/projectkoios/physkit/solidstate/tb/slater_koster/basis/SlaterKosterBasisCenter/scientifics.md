# `SlaterKosterBasisCenter` scientific contract

The center is an embedding label for represented orbitals, not an atom, species, Wannier center, or symmetry site unless a client establishes that meaning.

The represented relation

$$
c\longmapsto\tau_c\in\mathbb R^3.
$$

is a mathematical or structural software contract. Passing construction and tests
establishes conformance to that contract only; it does not establish material accuracy,
parameter acceptance, convergence, independent verification, or experimental agreement.

It does not own lattice vectors, Cartesian positions, species, occupancy, or symmetry data.
