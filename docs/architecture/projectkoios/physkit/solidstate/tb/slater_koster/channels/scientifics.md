# Slater–Koster channel-basis scientific contract

The channel matrices separate angular geometry from energy-valued coefficients. For
fixed $d$, the map

$$
V\in\mathbb R^7\longmapsto T(d;V)\in\mathbb R^{5\times5}
$$

is linear, and

$$
B_\alpha(d)=\frac{\partial T(d;V)}{\partial V_\alpha}.
$$

This derivative identity is algebraic. It does not imply that the seven parameters are
identifiable from a chosen dataset, statistically independent, physically transferable,
or sufficient for a material.

Direction reversal gives

$$
B_\alpha(-d)=B_\alpha(d)^{\mathsf T}
$$

for every admitted homopolar channel, supporting generated Hermitian-reverse bonds.
The relation depends on the represented orbital order and sign convention; it is not a
universal convention for all Slater–Koster tables.

The module makes no claim for heteropolar channels, $d$- or $f$-orbital interaction
tables, spin, spin–orbit coupling, overlap matrices, or generalized eigenproblems.
