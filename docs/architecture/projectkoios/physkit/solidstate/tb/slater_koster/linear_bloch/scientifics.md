# Linear Slater–Koster Bloch scientific contract

The module evaluates a declared linear finite model family at declared reciprocal
coordinates. It does not choose the coordinates, declare them training or validation
samples, or compare the output to another operator.

Ordinary-cell periodicity is a representation property. A full-center representation
requires the separate orbital-center gauge transformation and is generally
reciprocal-gauge covariant rather than elementwise periodic.

No fitted vector, residual, spectrum, observable, uncertainty, material adequacy,
scientific validation, or human acceptance follows from successful evaluation or
reconstruction tests.
