# `SlaterKosterBondDirection` scientific contract

Its components enter the represented formulas as direction cosines. The record does not own the physical bond length.

The represented relation

$$
d=(l,m,n),\qquad \lVert d\rVert_2=1.
$$

is a mathematical or structural software contract. Passing construction and tests
establishes conformance to that contract only; it does not establish material accuracy,
parameter acceptance, convergence, independent verification, or experimental agreement.

It does not infer direction from integer displacement, center labels, or lattice geometry.
