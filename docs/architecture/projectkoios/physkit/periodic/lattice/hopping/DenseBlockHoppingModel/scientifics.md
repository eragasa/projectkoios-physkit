# `DenseBlockHoppingModel` scientific contract

The model identifies a finite dense-block representation of a translation-invariant
lattice Hamiltonian in one ordered primitive-cell basis and energy convention.
Displacements belong to the ordinary integer lattice.

It does not impose conjugate closure, lattice symmetry, a finite-DFT modulus,
truncation adequacy, fitting status, or material validation.
