# `BaseBlockHoppingModel` scientific contract

The abstraction correlates one represented hopping inventory with lattice
dimension, ordered basis identity, energy unit, and energy reference. It preserves
ordinary-lattice displacement semantics and requires all terms to act on one
retained state space.

It does not identify a material, infer symmetry or Hermiticity, impose finite range,
or classify the model as fitted, verified, or validated.
