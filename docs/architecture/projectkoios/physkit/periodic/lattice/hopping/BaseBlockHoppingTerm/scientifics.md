# `BaseBlockHoppingTerm` scientific contract

The abstraction states what every matrix-valued hopping coefficient must identify:
an ordinary-lattice displacement, one square retained-basis block, one energy unit,
and its storage representation. It does not prescribe dense or sparse storage.

It does not infer Hermiticity, a conjugate partner, an onsite condition, a cutoff,
or a physical interpretation for the retained basis.
