# `BlockHoppingModel` scientific contract

The factory selects a represented storage family for one already specified hopping
model. Selection preserves the model identity, lattice dimension, energy reference,
basis identity, displacement inventory, and coefficient values.

The factory does not choose a physical model, basis, gauge, range, fitting policy,
or scientific acceptance criterion.
