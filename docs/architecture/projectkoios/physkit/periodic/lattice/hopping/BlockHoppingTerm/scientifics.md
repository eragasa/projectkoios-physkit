# `BlockHoppingTerm` scientific contract

The factory chooses software representation only. Dense and sparse products
represent the same kind of scientific object, $T(\mathbf R)$, and preserve the
supplied displacement, basis block, and unit exactly.

Factory selection does not establish Hermiticity, locality quality, physical
provenance, or model adequacy.
