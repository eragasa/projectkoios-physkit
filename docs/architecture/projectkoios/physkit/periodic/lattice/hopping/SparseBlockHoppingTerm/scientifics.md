# `SparseBlockHoppingTerm` scientific contract

The record represents one internally sparse retained-basis coefficient
$T(\mathbf R)$. Sparse storage concerns matrix entries inside the block; spatial
support remains identified independently by the displacement inventory.

Sparse storage does not itself establish a compact spatial model, approximation
quality, Hermiticity, or scientific validity.
