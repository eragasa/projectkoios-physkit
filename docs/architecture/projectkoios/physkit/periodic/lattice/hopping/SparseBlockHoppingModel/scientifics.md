# `SparseBlockHoppingModel` scientific contract

The model identifies a finite ordinary-lattice inventory whose retained-basis blocks
use sparse internal storage. Internal sparsity and spatial support are distinct model
properties.

It does not claim that the Bloch Hamiltonian remains sparse for every reciprocal
point, that the representation is spatially compact, or that the model is fitted or
validated.
